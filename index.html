    About Rovince Nyagwang

    Early Life Rovince Otieno Nyagwang’, born on 1st September 2010, is a young Luo gospel artist from Ndhiwa, Homa Bay County, Kenya. He is the son of Bradocks Onyango Nyagwang’ and Lilian Atieno Onyango, whose strong parental foundation in Christ played a significant role in shaping his faith and inspiring his journey into gospel music at a tender age. Growing up in a Christ-centered family, Rovince developed a love for God, a passion for worship, and a desire to use his musical talent to spread the Gospel of Jesus Christ. Inspired by the Christian values instilled in him by his parents, he embraced gospel artistry as a meaningful way to express his faith, encourage others, and share messages of hope, love, and salvation through music.

    Education Education and Musical Journey Rovince began his education at Shauri Yako Primary School in Homa Bay Town, where he nurtured his passion for music and released his first song, titled “Ngima To Tek.” This marked an important milestone in his journey as a young Luo gospel artist. He later joined Nyamanga Mixed Day Boarding School, where he is currently pursuing his Form Four studies while continuing to develop his musical talent and commitment to spreading the Gospel through music.

    Achievements Rovince is a promising young Luo gospel artist balancing education and music. His second song, “Genona,” marked an important milestone in his musical journey. Through gospel music, he aspires to inspire his generation, share the message of Christ, and glorify God through his talent.
